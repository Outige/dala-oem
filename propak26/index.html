For prospective suppliers

Matching the pack
is only the start.

We’re looking for manufacturers and distributors who can supply comparable packaging reliably and consistently.

01

Match the physical sample

Capacity, neck finish, closure fit, wall weight and overall dimensions must be confirmed against a physical Dala sample.

02

Confirm compatibility

Materials and closures should be suitable for the intended paint, glue, medium, dough or creative product formulation.

03

Quote the complete supply

Include container, closure, liner or seal where relevant, MOQ, lead time, tooling, colour options and delivery terms.

04

Send production samples

Digital images are visual references only. Dala will test real samples before approving any replacement format.
